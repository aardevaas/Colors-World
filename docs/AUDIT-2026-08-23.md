Tab stops per room: library 263 · compose 21 · scales 16 · visualizer 24 ·

Library's 263 is the virtualised colour grid and is expected; the two grids that
were pathological — library's family ramps (600 stops) and typography's
legibility field (81) — both now use a roving tabindex.

### What the pass actually fixed

- **The product contradicted itself about its central claim.** Compose graded 4
  hand-picked pairs, Visualizer graded 11. Over 400 solved palettes, **240 of
  240** that Compose called green had a failure Visualizer would report. One
  matrix now, verified identical to the last digit.
- **The audit was unsatisfiable by construction.** `surface on background` at
  3:1 failed on 400/400 generated palettes and on the shipped fallback. Made
  advisory; the border requirement (3:1 against *both* panel and page) carries
  the intent. Clean palettes went **0/400 → 296/400**.
- **`#0A0A0B` was never an extreme.** It parses to OKLCH lightness 0.145 and
  left **2.1% of in-gamut fills unservable** — plain `#3E7CB1` measured
  4.4506:1 on a pair the role model guarantees cannot fail. `INK_DARK` is
  `#000000`; worst case over the gamut is now 4.5826:1.
- **An overridden fill kept the previous fill's ink**, so the room offered an
  "auto-fix" for an unfailable pair.
- **Studio's canvas had collapsed to zero height**, so every card the room
  created landed 480px above the window. Clicking "+ note" did nothing visible.
- **Library's 90 lightness ticks were unreachable by mouse** — an invisible
  overlay with `pointer-events: auto` sat on top of them.

## 5 · The surface outside the six rooms

### 5.1 The landing CTA — the one real defect

The prism button's label is white over an animated multi-colour pill. Its
**worst local contrast is 1.24:1**, and *the worst point moves between frames*
as the white filament crosses the baseline. At 15px/600 it needs 4.5.

This sits on the paint/prism showpiece, which is deliberate and protected. It is
a **design decision, not a bug fix** — options are a text scrim, dropping the
filament below the baseline, or accepting it. Not mine to pick.

Two other flags on this page were the instrument, not the product: text painted
with `background-clip: text` has `-webkit-text-fill-color: transparent`, so a
nominal-colour reading reports 1:1. Off rendered pixels the headline accent is
**18.29:1** and "aardevaas" is **6.87:1**.

### 5.2 Structural gaps — 4 routes

`/assets` has no `<main>` element at all. `/login`, `/palettes` and `/merge` are
each missing some combination of `<main>`, nav, header and skip link. All six
rooms got these in the pass; these four never did.

### 5.3 Orphans

`grep` finds **zero inbound links** to `/merge`, `/assets`, and `/library/[id]`.
The last is the notable one: a complete colour-detail page with a contrast audit
and semantic tags, unreachable because library cards open a drawer instead.

## 6 · Data and infrastructure

**Supabase** — 6 SQL files: `schema.sql` (15 KB), `policies.sql` (10 KB),
`enable-rls.sql` (8 KB), plus `sharing`, `storage`, `brand-assets`.
`enable-rls.sql` is **unrunnable past the first user** — it opens with a
bootstrap that `raise exception`s unless exactly one profile exists, which
aborts the script before any policy executes. `policies.sql` is the idempotent
policy layer and is the one that can actually be applied.

**Security headers** are set in `next.config.mjs`: `nosniff` and
`strict-origin-when-cross-origin` globally, plus `DENY`/`frame-ancestors 'none'`
/`no-referrer` on `/share/*`. There is **no Content-Security-Policy** on the app
itself.

**No CI.** No `.github/` at all — no workflow, no issue templates, no PR
template. The verification standard in the docs ("tsc clean, suite green, prod
build clean") is entirely manual and unenforced.

**Missing community files:** `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`,
`SECURITY.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `.nvmrc`, `.editorconfig`.

## 7b · Product-level findings — added 2026-08-23 after the vision correction

The audit above measured *quality*. These measure *fitness for the product's
actual purpose*, which changed when the founder corrected the vision from
"colour tool" to "brand-book platform". All four were reproduced live.

**There is no brand-book object.** Zero references to a brand book, guideline
or style guide across 33,506 lines. Six rooms produce inputs to an artifact
that does not exist. This is the largest gap in the product.

| Room | Measured |
|---|---|
| `/compose` | **Zero** export / copy / save / download affordances. The only exit is "Apply to System". A user who came for a palette cannot finish. The exporters exist in `src/lib/exporters/` and are simply not wired here. |
| `/scales` | **208 controls** for a six-colour palette across **3,971px** of scroll — four screens. 33 controls per panel, 15 of them curve control points across 3 SVG editors. |
| `/scales` | **The star is a dead control.** It moves the marker and re-derives names but never writes `system.anchorHex`. Verified: star index 0 → 2, reload → back to 0, storage unchanged. Every other room reads that anchor, so starring propagates nowhere. |
| `/scales` | Palette × colour, not a palette system — each swatch gets an independent ramp with its own settings; there are no shared steps or palette-level tokens. |
| `/visualizer` | Stage **hard-fixed at 760×475px**: **23%** of a 1600px viewport, **10%** of a 2560px one. It never grows. Inspector beside it is 320px. |
| `/typography` | **4 hardcoded pairings**, plus `queryLocalFonts()` — Chromium-only and permission-gated, so most visitors see four options. |

**Font source, verified live** — `https://api.fontsource.org/v1/fonts`, no API
key: **2,096 families** (1,976 Google + 120 other), 565 variable, 789 sans /
481 display / 365 serif / 364 handwriting / 72 mono. Licences: 2,052 OFL-1.1,
36 Apache-2.0, rest CC0/MIT/UFL — **all open, and the licence is in the
metadata**, which brand-book §9 requires anyway.

## 9 · What is genuinely world-class here

Worth stating, because the audit above is all problems and that is a distorted
picture:

- **The colour engine.** OKLCH throughout, sRGB/P3/Rec2020 + print mapping,
  WCAG 2.x *and* APCA, four CVD models, ΔE-OK. 1,368 LOC with 952 LOC of tests.
  MIT-licensed and genuinely rare as a dependency.
- **The role model.** One `deriveRoles` shared by every room, ordered by
  perceptual lightness, with an ink that is derived from its fill and provably
  legible across the entire sRGB gamut.
- **The solver.** Contrast requirements as *input* rather than something you
  roll dice against — and it explains what it could not reach and why.
- **The rigour.** 935 tests, 86.65% coverage, zero TODOs, zero dead files, and
  a comment culture that records *why* rather than *what*.

## 10 · The instruments

The measurement rig lives in the session scratchpad and is worth rebuilding
rather than re-deriving. Recorded in project memory: seven distinct ways it has
produced false findings, including the two that cost the most —

- **Darkest-vs-lightest pixel is wrong in both directions.** It under-reports
  small antialiased text (a caption at 4.00:1 against a nominal 5.82:1) *and*
  reports 1:1 for a short text run in a large box, because both extremes sampled
  are background. The latter invented a "genuine invisible text" finding that
  measures 5.74:1.
- **Nominal foreground is wrong for painted text** — `background-clip: text`
  reports `rgba(0,0,0,0)`.

The rule: **nominal foreground vs modal background for ordinary text; rendered
pixels when `-webkit-text-fill-color` is transparent or an ancestor carries a
filter or blend mode.** Neither ruler is right for both.
